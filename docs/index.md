# Quilly – transcripts for Google Meet

Quilly is a Chrome extension that records Google Meet's native captions into a local transcript,
lets you export it, lets you ask an AI model questions about it through your own OpenRouter key, and
lets local AI agents such as Claude Code read it through the `quilly-mcp` server. Everything stays
in your browser unless you explicitly send a transcript to OpenRouter or switch on local agents, which
read it over a connection on your own computer.
