# Quilly – transcripts for Google Meet

Quilly is a Chrome extension that records Google Meet's native captions into a local transcript,
lets you export it, and lets you ask an AI model questions about it through your own OpenRouter key.
Everything stays in your browser unless you explicitly send a transcript to OpenRouter.
